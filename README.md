# Northwind Traders Sales Analytics Capstone

## 📁 Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive sales analytics solution for the fictional **Northwind Traders** company. By leveraging data visualization and exploratory data analysis (EDA), this solution uncovers meaningful insights across the sales cycle—from procurement and inventory management to employee performance and customer retention—to support data-driven business strategy.

## 📌 Key Features
* **Sales Trend Analysis:** Monthly and yearly revenue breakdowns to identify seasonality and growth patterns.
* **Customer Segmentation:** Analysis of customer behavior and geographical distribution to optimize marketing efforts.
* **Product Performance:** Insights into category-level revenue and inventory health (stock levels vs. demand).
* **Regional Metrics:** Mapping sales performance by country and city to identify underperforming markets.
* **Employee Evaluation:** Tracking sales representative performance, order volume, and tenure.
* **Supply Chain Efficiency:** Monitoring supplier reliability and order processing timelines.

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for complex calculations.
* **SQL:** Data querying and relational database management.
* **Excel:** Initial data cleaning and preprocessing.

## 🏁 Project Conclusions

### 👥 Customer Distribution & Behavior
* **Market Concentration:** EDA revealed that most customers are located in North America and Western Europe, with the USA and Germany leading in customer count and sales.
* **Retention:** Power BI visualizations showed that repeat customers contribute over **70% of total revenue**, indicating strong brand loyalty.
* **Trends:** Acquisition trends show steady growth with periodic spikes aligned with holiday seasons.

### 📈 Sales Performance Insights
* **Seasonality:** Revenue analysis identified **Q3 as the peak sales period**, likely driven by seasonal demand.
* **Representative Impact:** The top 3 sales representatives contribute to over **40% of total company sales**, highlighting a significant reliance on high-performing staff.

### 📦 Product & Inventory Trends
* **Revenue Drivers:** "Beverages" and "Condiments" consistently rank as the highest revenue-generating categories.
* **Inventory Gaps:** Analysis detected high-demand products frequently going out of stock, while low-performing items maintain high stock levels (potential deadstock).

### 🚢 Supplier & Shipping Insights
* **Reliability:** While key suppliers consistently deliver on time, others show high variability, causing delays in high-value orders.
* **Operational Bottlenecks:** Late shipments were found to correlate with specific shipping regions, suggesting a need for logistics optimization.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
* **Retention Marketing:** Use targeted campaigns to convert one-time buyers into repeat customers.
* **Inventory Optimization:** Implement predictive analytics to reduce overstock and ensure high-demand products remain available.
* **Regional Growth:** Focus on underperforming regions with localized incentives or training for regional sales teams.
* **Vendor Management:** Strengthen relationships with reliable suppliers and reevaluate contracts with those causing frequent shipment delays.
